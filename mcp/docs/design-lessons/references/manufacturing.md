# Manufacturing lessons (JLCPCB)

- **Check stock and lifecycle when choosing parts, and again on the order day.** A whole modem family turned out to be end-of-life and forced a redesign; a charger had zero stock on the order day; a connector had stock for about 20 boards. LCSC product data has a lifecycle field and alternatives; flag single-source parts.
- **Each distinct Extended part adds a fixed fee per order.** Consolidate values, propose Basic alternatives, and revert substitutes to Basic parts when stock returns.
- **Export BOM and CPL in JLCPCB format with LCSC codes**, and export again after every change; otherwise the part-number column is empty. Confirm or assign unmatched parts in the assembly tool. Warnings for DNP footprints missing from the BOM are expected.
- **DNP means "add to BOM" turned off (`addIntoBom = false`) in both schematic and PCB.** A "DNP" value text is not a DNP flag, and turning it off only on the PCB makes DRC report a schematic/PCB mismatch. Test points and DNP parts must be absent from both BOM and CPL.
- **Before export, diff schematic and PCB attributes.** The CPL device column still carried old MPNs when PCB attributes had not been updated, and a PCB value differed from the schematic (it could have mounted an old 6.3 V capacitor).
- **Compare the project's DRC rules and footprint vias with the fab's current capabilities**, and record the date checked. Seen: a global track gap of 0.102 mm below the 0.127 mm minimum; module footprint vias with too small an annular ring; small drills with small pads costing extra. Copper-to-edge minimums differ between sources: check the current capability page.
- **Calibrate the imported CPL before trusting it**: compare several separated, asymmetric parts (connectors and modules near opposite corners, every package with a known origin or angle exception) between the EasyEDA placement and the assembler's import, to catch a constant offset, a swapped axis, mirroring or a bottom-side convention. `operate-jlcpcb-order` in [pcba-design-skills](https://github.com/Keitark/pcba-design-skills) describes the full review.
- **Review polarity and rotation in the assembler's preview**: parts without a pin-1 mark (SOT-23), LEDs whose footprint bar marks the anode, polarized capacitors after footprint changes, module and connector orientation.
- **Through-hole parts add cost and lead time**: decide on hand soldering early.
- **Plan parts the assembler does not stock** (HV magnetics, an isolated RJ45): buy and hand-solder, or consign. Consigned and assembler stock cannot be mixed for the same part.
